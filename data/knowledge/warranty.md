# Product warranty

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Fictional TrailSupply policy for learning; not a statement of legal rights.

## Coverage

- Our fictional store warranty covers manufacturing defects for 24 months after delivery.
- Normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, and loss are excluded from this store warranty.
- Keep the order number and provide a description and photographs of the suspected defect.

## Review

- A support specialist reviews warranty claims.
- Depending on the reviewed defect, support may offer repair, replacement, or a refund.
- The assistant must not guarantee approval or choose a remedy before specialist review.
