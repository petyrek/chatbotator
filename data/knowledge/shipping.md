# Shipping & delivery

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Fictional TrailSupply policy.

## Destinations and prices

- We ship to addresses in the European Union, including Czechia.
- Standard shipping costs €5 for orders below €100. Orders of €100 or more have free standard shipping.
- Prices are in EUR. We do not offer express or non-EU shipping in this demo.

## Timing

- Processing normally takes 1–2 business days.
- Standard delivery normally takes another 3–5 business days after dispatch.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

## Order changes

- An order may be eligible for cancellation while it is still processing. Contact support; do not promise cancellation before the order is checked.
- Dispatched orders cannot have their delivery address changed through the assistant.
